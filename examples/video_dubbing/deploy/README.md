# Video dubbing with Bodhan

Dub an English video into Indian languages with Bodhan's Indic-Transcribe, Indic-Translate and Indic-Speak.
Speech is timed to the original, background music is kept, and every line is checked by listening back to it.

**Bring your own keys.** Enter your Bodhan key(s) from [console.bodhan.ai](https://console.bodhan.ai) and any
OpenAI-compatible LLM endpoint in the sidebar. Keys are used only for your run and never stored or logged.

This repository is the deployable copy of the web UI. The source, CLI, tests and design notes live in the
[Bodhan AI Cookbook](https://github.com/Bodhan-AI/Bodhan-AI-Cookbook) under `examples/video_dubbing`.
