## Chapter 6: Lines and Angles

### 6.1 Introduction

A line has length but no breadth. A point has no dimensions.
Two lines that never meet are called parallel lines.

## 6.2 Angles

An angle is formed when two rays share an end point.

A right angle measures 90 degrees. A straight angle measures 180 degrees

## 6.3 Summary

Vertically opposite angles are equal.

The angles of a linear pair add up to 180 degrees.
