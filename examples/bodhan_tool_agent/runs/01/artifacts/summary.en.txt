This chapter introduces the basic concepts of lines and angles. A line extends indefinitely in both directions and has length but no width, while a point has no dimensions. Parallel lines are defined as lines that never intersect. Angles are formed when two rays share a common endpoint; a right angle measures 90 degrees and a straight angle measures 180 degrees. The chapter also notes that vertically opposite angles are equal, and the two angles that make up a linear pair sum to 180 degrees. These fundamental ideas provide a foundation for further study of geometric relationships and calculations.
