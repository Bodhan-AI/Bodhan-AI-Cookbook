Chapter 3: How Plants Make Food

Green plants make their own food by photosynthesis.
Leaves take in carbon dioxide from the air and water from
the roots. Sunlight gives the energy. The green pigment
chlorophyll traps that energy.

$$1$$

3.1 The leaf

A leaf has tiny pores called stomata. A single leaf can have
more than 10,000 stomata. They open in the day and close
at night to save water.

3.2 Water and minerals

Roots absorb water and minerals from the soil. Xylem tubes
carry them up the stem to the leaves. A tall tree can lift
water more than 100 metres.

3.3 The equation

Carbon dioxide + water -> glucose + oxygen. Six molecules
of carbon dioxide and six of water make one molecule of
glucose and six of oxygen.

3.4 Storing food

Plants store extra glucose as starch in roots, stems and
seeds. Potatoes and rice are rich in starch.

3.5 Why it matters

Almost all food chains begin with photosynthesis. Animals
eat plants, and other animals eat them. Plants also release
the oxygen we breathe.
