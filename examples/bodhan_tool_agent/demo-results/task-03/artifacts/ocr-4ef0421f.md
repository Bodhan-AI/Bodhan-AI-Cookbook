1. What is an Interjection? (Definition)

An Interjection is a "Feeling Word"! It is a short, exciting word that shows
sudden feelings like happiness, sadness, anger, or surprise.

$\bullet$ **The Golden Rule**: Interjections always use an Exclamation Mark ($!$) right
after them.

2. The Interjection Chart (Types & Examples)

| Feeling | Interjection Word | Easy Example Sentences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Happiness & Joy | Wow! / Yay! | • Yay! We won the game!<br>• Wow! This painting is beautiful! |
| Sadness | Oh no! / Alas! | • Oh no! My toy is broken.<br>• Alas! The ice cream dropped. |
| Surprise | Oh! / Ah! | • Oh! I did not see you there.<br>• Ah! Now I understand. |
| Pain or Hurt | Ouch! | • Ouch! The sharp pin hurt my finger. |
| Silence | Shh! | • Shh! The baby is sleeping. |
| Disgust (Yucky things) | Yuck! | • Yuck! This mud is sticky and dirty. |

Examples

$\bullet$ **Wow!** That rocket launched so high into
space.
$\bullet$ **Ouch!** A sharp bee stung my little ankle.
$\bullet$ **Yay!** We are going to the amusement
park.
$\bullet$ **Oh no!** The milk spilled all over the
counter.
$\bullet$ **Shh!** Talk softly so you do not disturb
anyone.
$\bullet$ **Alas!** The old beautiful castle fell down
today.
$\bullet$ **Yuck!** This old food tastes completely
spoiled.
