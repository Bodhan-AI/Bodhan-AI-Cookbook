# Chapter 6: Lines and Angles & Properties Worksheet

When two lines intersect each other, the vertically opposite angles are equal. When a transversal intersects two parallel lines, each pair of corresponding angles is equal, and each pair of alternate interior angles is equal. The sum of the three interior angles of any triangle is always 180 degrees.
