Bees live together in large groups called colonies. Each colony has a queen bee, several hundred male bees, and thousands of worker bees. The worker bees collect nectar and pollen from flowers. As they move from flower to flower, they carry pollen, which helps plants produce seeds and fruits. This is called pollination. About one-third of the food we eat depends on the pollination by bees and other insects. Farmers sometimes place beehives near their fields to get a good harvest. Excessive use of chemical pesticides can kill bees, so many farmers are now choosing natural methods to protect their crops.
