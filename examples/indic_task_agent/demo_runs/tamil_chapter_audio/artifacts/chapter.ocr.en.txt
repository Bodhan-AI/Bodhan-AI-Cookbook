## Water and Our Village

Every village depends on water.
Wells, ponds, and rivers
give people water to drink and produce food. When there is little rain, the whole village must share it carefully.

$$1$$

---

## Where Does Rain Come From

Water vapor from the sea forms clouds. When the clouds cool down, it rains. The rain fills up the rivers and soaks into the ground.

$$2$$

---

## Keeping Water Clean

Dirty water spreads disease. Wells should be covered, hands washed, and waste not thrown in the pond. Boiling water makes it safe to drink.

$$3$$

---

## Saving Every Drop

A dripping faucet can waste hundreds of liters a month. Turning off taps, reusing water for plants, and collecting rainwater from roofs help the village.

$$4$$

---

## The Village Well

Once upon a time, the village well was where people met every day. Today many houses have pipes, but when the pipes run dry, the well still provides water.

5

---

## Farmers and Water

Farmers need water at the right time. Some crops like rice need more water. Others like millet need very little. Choosing the right crop helps save water.

$$6$$

---

## Rivers and Floods

During the rainy season, a river can rise quickly. Villages near rivers should watch the water level and move to higher ground when the river floods.

$$7$$

---

## What We Learned

Water is precious. We learned where it comes from, how to keep it clean, and how to save it. Each one of us can protect our village's water.
