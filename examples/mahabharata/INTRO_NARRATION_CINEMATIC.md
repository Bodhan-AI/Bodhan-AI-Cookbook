# MAHABHARATA: The Living Epic — Cinematic Narration (TTS-ready)

**Narrator:** Krishna · **Length:** ~4.5 min
**How to use:** Everything in `[square brackets]` inside a spoken line is a voice-emotion tag for the TTS engine (ElevenLabs v3 style). Lines in *italics* are screen/sound cues. Don't voice those. "..." = a held pause.

---

## 1. ARRIVAL (0:00 – 0:40)

*[Black screen. Wind. A distant conch. Fireflies rise over Naimisha Forest.]*

[whispers] Welcome... traveller.

[long pause]

[softly, with a smile] You are wondering who is speaking.

[warm] I am Krishna. And for this journey... I am your narrator.

[low, steady] You are about to enter the Mahabharata. [pause] Not as a reader, standing outside the pages... but as a wanderer, walking *through* them.

[gently] Chapter by chapter. Door by door.

[confident, warm] I will take you through all of it.

---

## 2. THE FLOW OF TIME (0:40 – 1:45)

*[Camera glides from the forest to a river, then to palace gates. A chapter rail glows at the screen's edge. Strings rise slowly.]*

[reflective] Our story begins where it was first told. [pause] In Naimisha Forest... where sages sit beneath old trees, [hushed] and a storyteller begins to speak.

[building] And then, time moves.

[steady] Hastinapura... a throne. A river goddess. A vow so heavy, it bends a dynasty.

[quicker, rising] Young princes grow. Rivalry becomes resentment. A swayamvara. A new city, Indraprastha, rising from wasteland. [slows, dark] And then... a game of dice.

[sighs] [quietly] That is where a family breaks.

[grave] Exile. The long road to war. [pause] Kurukshetra. [whispers] The Gita. [firm] Eighteen days that no one forgets.

[exhales slowly] And after the war... [very soft] silence. The fall of Dwaraka. And at the very end... a final climb into the Himalaya.

[warm again] Thirty-two chapters. One long river of time. [inviting] Follow it in order... or step into any moment you choose.

---

## 3. THE PEOPLE (1:45 – 2:30)

*[Portraits materialize one by one in soft golden light. A single flute.]*

[tender] And what is an epic... without its people?

[admiring] You will meet Arjuna, the archer who lowered his bow. [warm chuckle] Bhima, whose strength is matched only by his appetite... and his heart. [respectful] Yudhishthira, who carries the weight of truth. [quietly] And Draupadi... whose question still echoes through the Dice Hall.

[deeper] Bhishma, prisoner of his own vow. Drona, the teacher. [soft, pained] Karna... the generous, and the wounded. [measured] And Duryodhana, whose ambition has a story of its own.

[gently] Fifty lives. Each with their own reasons.

[quiet, certain] Nobody here is simple. [pause] That is why they feel real.

---

## 4. THE PLACES (2:30 – 3:10)

*[The 3D map unfolds across the screen. Each place glows as it is named.]*

[awe] Open the map... and the world wakes.

[unhurried] Walk the streets of Hastinapura. Stand in the Maya Sabha at Indraprastha, [amused] where floors look like water... and water looks like floor.

[dropping low] Step into the Dice Hall, [pause] where the worst decision was made in the quietest room.

[softer] Rest in Kamyaka Forest, through the long exile. [proud, warm] Visit my own city, Dwaraka... shining by the sea.

[heavy, slow] And stand... at Kurukshetra. [pause] The field where everything was decided.

[whispers] Every place holds a memory. Every memory... holds a question.

---

## 5. "ARE YOU STUCK?" — OUR AI (3:10 – 4:25)

*[Music softens. A glowing panel slides in beside the story. A voice waveform pulses.]*

[knowing, gentle] But I know travellers. [pause] Sooner or later... you will stop, and wonder.

[thoughtful] *Why did Bhishma stay silent? Who was truly related to whom? What happened on the thirteenth day?*

[leaning in, reassuring] If you are ever stuck... do not turn back. [warm, firm] Ask.

[bright] Use our AI.

[clear, upbeat] It speaks many languages. [light] Say your question in your own tongue... or type it. Our speech recognition listens. [smile] And our text-to-speech answers, [softly] so you may simply close your eyes, and listen.

[building] But here is what makes it different.

[intrigued] Most AI gives you only words. [pause] Ours *shows* you the answer. [confident] With visualization tools made with AI, it draws family trees, timelines, relationship maps... [slower] you don't just read who stood where. You *see* it.

[serious, assured] And it does not guess. [pause] Behind it stands a Vector-Less Graph. [measured] No blur of numbers. No loose similarity. [firm] A real graph of the epic, [clear] people, places, events... connected exactly the way the story connects them.

[warm] Ask a question, and it walks those connections, step by step... [softly] and shows you the trail it followed.

[quiet emphasis] It doesn't just give you an answer. [pause] It shows you the path.

---

## 6. CLOSING (4:25 – 4:50)

*[Camera pulls back to the quiet forest. Conch, softer. Fireflies drift upward.]*

[honest, gentle] This epic has many tellings, and wise people have argued over them for ages. [pause] Where they differ... we will tell you.

[warm] Think of this voyage as an invitation to wonder... not a verdict.

[soft, close, almost a whisper] So, traveller... [pause] the forest is quiet. The story is ready. [smile] And I am beside you.

[long pause]

[warm, quiet] Shall we begin?

*[Hold on the forest. Slow fade into Chapter 1: Naimisharanya.]*

|---|---|

| Mood | `[tender]` `[grave]` `[awe]` `[amused]` `[reflective]` `[knowing]` `[pained]` |
| Pacing | `[pause]` `[long pause]` `[slows]` `[building]` `[unhurried]` |

**Notes**
- `[yawning]` is in the cheat-sheet but I left it out of the script. A yawn would break the divine, composed tone. If you want one, a playful line works best in section 5, such as "[yawns] Stuck at midnight? Even then... ask."
- Tag support varies by TTS engine. ElevenLabs v3 handles most of these. For other engines, run a test and trim the tags it reads aloud.
