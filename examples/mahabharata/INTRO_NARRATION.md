## 1. Arrival (0:00 – 0:35)

*[Dark screen. A single conch sounds, then forest ambience. Fireflies rise over Naimisha Forest.]*

Welcome, traveller.

Before you ask who is speaking… I will tell you.

I am Krishna. And for the next few moments, I am your narrator.

You are about to enter the Mahabharata. It is not a book you read from the outside. It is an epic you walk through, one chapter at a time. I will take you through all of it.

---

## 2. How the Voyage Flows (0:35 – 1:35)

*[Camera drifts from the forest to a river, then to palace gates. The chapter rail glows along the edge.]*

Our journey begins where the story was first told. In the quiet of Naimisha Forest, sages gather to listen… and a storyteller begins.

From there, time carries us forward.

We enter the kingdom of Hastinapura, where a river goddess, a vow, and a prince named Devavrata change the fate of a dynasty. We watch young princes grow. We see rivalry turn into resentment. We see the swayamvara of Draupadi, the rise of Indraprastha, and a single game of dice that breaks a family.

Then comes exile. Then comes the road to war. Then Kurukshetra, the Gita, and the eighteen days that no one forgets.

And after the war… the silence. The return to Hastinapura. The fall of Dwaraka. And at the very end, one last climb into the Himalaya.

Thirty-two chapters. One long river of time. You can follow it in order… or step into any moment you choose.

---

## 3. The People (1:35 – 2:20)

*[Portraits fade in one by one, each with a soft light.]*

And what is an epic without its people?

You will meet Arjuna, the archer who lowered his bow. Bhima, whose strength is matched only by his heart. Yudhishthira, who bears the weight of truth. And Draupadi, whose question still echoes through the Dice Hall.

You will meet Bhishma, bound by his own vow. Drona, the teacher. Karna, the generous and the wounded. And Duryodhana, whose ambition has a story of its own.

Fifty lives, each with their own reasons. Nobody here is simple. That is what makes them real.

---

## 4. The Places (2:20 – 3:00)

*[The 3D map unfolds. Locations light up as they are named.]*

Open the map, and the world comes alive.

Walk the streets of Hastinapura. Stand inside the Maya Sabha at Indraprastha, where floors look like water and water looks like floor. Enter the Dice Hall, where the worst decision was made in the quietest room. Rest in Kamyaka Forest during the long exile. Visit my own city of Dwaraka, shining by the sea.

And stand at Kurukshetra, the field where everything was decided.

Every place carries a memory. Every memory carries a question.

---

## 5. "Are You Stuck?" — Meet Our AI (3:00 – 4:20)

*[The scene softens. A glowing panel opens beside the story. Voice waveform pulses.]*

But I know something about travellers. Sooner or later… you will stop and wonder.

*Why did Bhishma stay silent? Who was truly related to whom? What really happened on the thirteenth day?*

If you are ever stuck, do not turn back. Ask.

Use our AI.

It understands many languages. Speak to it in yours, or type in yours. Our speech recognition listens, and our text-to-speech answers in a voice you can hear, so you can simply listen as the story unfolds.

But here is what makes it special.

Most AI answers with words alone. Ours also shows you the answer. It builds visualizations on the spot, with tools made with AI: family trees, timelines, relationship maps. You do not just read who stood where. You see it.

And it does not guess. Behind it stands a Vector-Less Graph. No blur of numbers, no loose similarity. It is a real graph of the epic: people, places and events, connected the way the story connects them. When you ask a question, our AI walks those connections, step by step, and shows you the trail it followed. Every answer can be traced back to its source.

That is the difference. It does not just say an answer. It shows you the path.

So, traveller… the forest is quiet. The story is ready. And I am beside you.
