# Sample data

Small, shareable inputs used by the getting-started notebooks.

| File | What it is | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `sample_hindi.wav` | ~6 s Hindi speech, 16 kHz mono PCM | `getting-started/speech-to-text/` |

Rules for adding files here:

- Under 1 MB each. Larger inputs belong in your recipe's own `sample_data/` folder and are git-ignored.
- No real personal data, identity documents, or recordings of people who have not consented to public use.
- Name files by content and language: `receipt_tamil.png`, `announcement_bengali.wav`.
